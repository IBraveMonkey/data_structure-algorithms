## 1. 💎 Game Monetization Models

### 💰 Premium/Paid
*   **Description:** Player pays once for the game
*   **Advantages:**
    *   No ads
    *   Full developer control
    *   Predictable revenue
*   **Disadvantages:**
    *   High barrier to entry for players
    *   Limited revenue after purchase
*   **Examples:** The Witcher 3, Portal 2, Stardew Valley

### 🎁 Free-to-Play (F2P)
*   **Description:** Game is free, revenue from in-game purchases
*   **Advantages:**
    *   Low barrier to entry
    *   Potentially high revenue
    *   Long-term support
*   **Disadvantages:**
    *   Perceived as "pay-to-win"
    *   Requires constant support
*   **Examples:** League of Legends, Fortnite, Candy Crush

**Example from Fortnite:**
Fortnite uses the Free-to-Play model with in-game purchases:
- **Battle Pass** — seasonal pass with rewards
- **Cosmetics** — skins, emotes, weapons (visual-only changes)
- **V-Bucks** — in-game currency for purchases
- **Seasonal content** — regular updates with new themes and events

### 🎟️ Subscription
*   **Description:** Monthly fee for game access
*   **Advantages:**
    *   Stable revenue
    *   Consistent audience
    *   Possibility of exclusive content
*   **Disadvantages:**
    *   Requires constant value
    *   Lower profit per player
*   **Examples:** World of Warcraft, Xbox Game Pass, EA Play

### 🛒 DLC/Expansions
*   **Description:** Additional content for money
*   **Advantages:**
    *   Increased value of base game
    *   Repeat purchases
    *   Additional story content
*   **Disadvantages:**
    *   May be perceived as "incomplete" game
    *   Requires additional development
*   **Examples:** The Sims expansions, Skyrim DLCs

### 📺 Advertising
*   **Description:** Revenue from showing ads in game
*   **Advantages:**
    *   Doesn't affect gameplay
    *   Passive income
    *   Suitable for casual games
*   **Disadvantages:**
    *   May annoy players
    *   Low cost per view
*   **Examples:** Angry Birds, AdCap games

## 2. 📱 Publishing Platforms

### 🖥️ PC Platforms:
*   **Steam** — largest PC platform
    *   Moderation: minimal
    *   Commission: 30% (lower with high sales)
    *   Features: community, reviews, workshop
*   **Epic Games Store** — Steam competitor
    *   Commission: 12% for developers
    *   Features: exclusives, free games
*   **GOG** — DRM-free platform
    *   Features: focus on older games, no DRM
*   **Itch.io** — indie game platform
    *   Features: low barrier to entry, experimental games support

### 🎮 Consoles:
*   **PlayStation Store** — for PS4/PS5
    *   Requirements: certification, compliance with standards
*   **Xbox Marketplace** — for Xbox
    *   Requirements: ID@Xbox program for indie developers
*   **Nintendo eShop** — for Switch
    *   Requirements: strict moderation, Nintendo business model

### 📱 Mobile Platforms:
*   **App Store (iOS)** — Apple
    *   Commission: 30% (15% after first year of subscription)
    *   Moderation: strict, lengthy
*   **Google Play Store** — Google
    *   Commission: 30% (15% for first $1M revenue)
    *   Moderation: less strict, faster

### 🌐 Web Platforms:
*   **HTML5 platforms** — Kongregate, Newgrounds
    *   Features: free games, advertising, ratings
*   **Browser-based** — itch.io, GameJolt
    *   Features: easy access, experimental projects

## 5. 📊 Analytics and Metrics

### 📈 Key metrics:
*   **DAU/MAU (Daily/Monthly Active Users)**
    *   Daily/monthly active users
    *   Engagement indicator
*   **Retention Rate**
    *   % of players returning after 1, 7, 30 days
    *   Critically important metric
*   **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)**
    *   Average revenue per user
    *   Monetization indicator
*   **Conversion Rate**
    *   % of players making purchases
    *   Monetization effectiveness

**Example from Fortnite:**
Fortnite tracks the following metrics:
- **DAU/MAU** — to assess audience activity
- **Retention Rate** — to evaluate the quality of the gaming experience
- **ARPU** — to evaluate monetization effectiveness
- **Conversion Rate** — to optimize Battle Pass and cosmetic purchases

### 🛠️ Analytics tools:
